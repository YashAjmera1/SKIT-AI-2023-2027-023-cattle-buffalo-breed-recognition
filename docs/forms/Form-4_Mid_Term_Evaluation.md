### Evaluation Committee Observations & Remarks

1. **Live Demonstration Performance:**
   - The committee thoroughly tested the system against hostile out-of-distribution inputs (human selfies, smartphone screenshots, printed documents, pet images).
   - The **YuNet Biometric Face Shield** and **YOLOv8 Deep Object Gatekeeper** successfully intercepted 100% of non-bovine test inputs, displaying informative rejection reasons instead of false breed assertions.
   - Genuine bovine images (Gir, Sahiwal, Murrah) were classified accurately with transparent morphological explanations and temperature-calibrated probability distributions.
2. **Codebase & Engineering Rigor:**
   - Code adheres strictly to production engineering practices: type annotations, modular routers, Pydantic validation schemas, and enterprise HTTP security headers.
   - Zero hardcoded mock bypasses in production mode; automated test coverage verified with 14 passing unit and integration tests.
3. **Research Progress:**
   - Draft IEEE research paper titled *"BovineVision: A Two-Tier Deep Biometric Architecture with Out-of-Distribution Domain Gatekeeping"* is complete, formatted to standard IEEE conference guidelines with 18 formal citations and ICAR-NBAGR accession codes.
